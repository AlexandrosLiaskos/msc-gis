Best-Map Competition — “Mapping the World”

07.05.2025·News

Student cartography competition organised in collaboration with the ArcGIS user community.

The Master's Programme in Geographic Information Systems invites all students of the Department of Geology and Geoenvironment — undergraduate and postgraduate alike — to take part in the first best-map competition, “Mapping the World”.

Entrants are asked to produce a map from data supplied to the participants — for the most part ready-made datasets — on the ArcGIS Pro platform, to which all students of the Department have access. The evaluation criteria, as defined by the competition's three-member committee, are set out in the relevant document.

The maps will be judged by the competition's three-member committee: Spyros Staridas, internationally recognised for his contribution to geoinformatics and cartography; Charalampos Kranis, Associate Professor of Tectonic Geology and Geological Mapping at NKUA; and Anna Karkani, member of the NKUA laboratory teaching staff (EDIP) in geomorphology and natural disasters, whose teaching subjects include cartography and visualisation.

Prizes will be awarded to the three best entries: a free seminar offered by Marathon Data Systems, a company with more than forty years of specialisation in Geographic Information Systems; publication of the winning maps on the Programme's social media and on the official websites of the Department and of the Programme; and printing and exhibition of the winning maps in the premises of the Department of Geology and Geoenvironment.

Participation forms, signed via gov.gr, are submitted by Friday 30 May 2025 to the Programme's email address. The competition runs for three months from its start date.
