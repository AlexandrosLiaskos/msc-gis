Cake-cutting ceremony 2025 (Κοπή Πίτας)

16.01.2025·Event

The programme's annual new-year gathering for current students, alumni, and teaching staff.

The Master's Programme in Geographic Information Systems extends an invitation to the annual new-year cake-cutting ceremony, to be held on Sunday 19 January 2025 at 12:00 in the Museum of Palaeontology and Geology of the Department of Geology and Geoenvironment, NKUA.

Guest speaker at the event is Mr Ioannis Kontos, General Director of Marathon Data Systems, the company that represents the ArcGIS Pro software in Greece.

The ceremony offers the members of the programme's community an occasion to meet in person and exchange ideas. All are welcome.
