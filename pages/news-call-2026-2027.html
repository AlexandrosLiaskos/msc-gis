Call for applications — academic year 2026–2027

26.05.2026·News

Applications for the next intake are open. See who can apply, the required documents, and how selection works.

The Faculty of Geology and Geoenvironment of the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens announces the call for applications to the Master's Programme in Geographic Information Systems for the academic year 2026–2027.

The Programme focuses on programming tools and databases in a GIS environment, online tools for commercial applications, and research topics.

Applications are submitted by email to the Programme's Secretariat from 18 May 2026 until 11 September 2026 (extended until 25 September 2026):

gis@geol.uoa.gr
