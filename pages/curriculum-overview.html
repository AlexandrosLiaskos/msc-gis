01

Teaching methodology

The “Geographic Information Systems” postgraduate programme begins in the winter semester of each academic year.

Courses are conducted exclusively through distance-learning methods. The synchronous distance-learning classes take place on Saturdays, so that working professionals can combine study with their weekday commitments.

A full breakdown of the courses offered each semester — with credits, learning outcomes, and detailed descriptions — is available in the course catalogue.

02

Duration of studies

Standard duration
4 semesters
Maximum duration
6 semesters
Suspension of study
up to 2 consecutive semesters

The duration of studies, which lead to the award of the Postgraduate Diploma, is set at four (4) academic semesters.

The maximum time allowed for the completion of studies is set at six (6) academic semesters, following a request by the student and a decision from the Assembly of the Department.

Postgraduate students may request a reasoned suspension of study. Semesters of suspension are not counted in the maximum duration of regular study. Such suspensions concern full academic semesters and may not exceed two consecutive semesters.

03

Progression and assessment

Progression from one semester to the next depends on successful completion of the prescribed coursework, with credits awarded per course according to the ECTS framework. The rules governing examinations, grading, and the standards required to graduate are set out on the assessment page.
