Welcome event 2023–2024 — held Saturday 14 October 2023

16.10.2023·Event

Opening gathering for the incoming cohort, with introductions to the teaching team and the year ahead.

The welcome event for the incoming students of the Master's Programme in Geographic Information Systems was held on Saturday 14 October 2023. During the event, students, teaching staff and members of the Coordinating Committee introduced themselves, marking the start of the academic year 2023–2024.

The Programme thanks all who honoured the event with their presence, and in particular the President of the Department of Geology and Geoenvironment of the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, Professor Assimina Antonarakou, the Vice-President of the Programme, Professor Niki Evelpidou, and the members of the Coordinating Committee, among them Professor Charalampos Kranis, as well as the Programme's teaching staff.

The Programme reaffirmed its commitment to its educational mission — the provision of in-depth knowledge of geospatial data and the use of state-of-the-art technologies for data management, modelling and visualisation — and to the continued support of its students throughout their studies.
