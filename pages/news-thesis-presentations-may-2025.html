Invitation to the presentation of Master theses

31.05.2025·Event

Master thesis presentations by the first graduating students of the programme — open to anyone interested.

All interested are invited to attend the presentation of the Master theses of the first graduating students of the Master's Programme in Geographic Information Systems.

The topics are focused on GIS, and the methods and issues analysed are of great interest and can offer both knowledge and inspiration.

The presentations take place online on Saturday 31 May 2025, from 10:00 to 11:30, via Webex, according to the following programme.

10:00 — Panagiotis Dimopoulos

“Geospatial modeling of surficial water retreat at the area of Thessaly, after Daniel storm event”

Examination committee: Emmanuel Vassilakis, Niki Evelpidou, Michalis Diakakis

Webex link

10:30 — Ioannis Apostolou

“The Impact of Climate Change on Energy Systems: A Geospatial Big Data Analysis Approach”

Examination committee: Konstantinos Philippopoulos, Konstantinos Kartalis, Ilias Agathangelidis

Webex link

11:00 — Evangelos Papastefanou

“Flood Analysis and Risk Assessment using Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) Data in Google Earth Engine for the Thessaly region”

Examination committee: Ilias Agathangelidis, Konstantinos Philippopoulos, Niki Evelpidou

Webex link
