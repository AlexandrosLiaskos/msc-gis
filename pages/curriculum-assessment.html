01

Assessment methods

The evaluation of students and their performance in the courses they attend within the framework of the programme is carried out at the end of each semester, through written or oral examinations, coursework produced across the semester, intermediate progress tests, written assignments, laboratory exercises, or a combination of the above.

02

Examination periods

The academic year includes three (3) examination weeks. Courses from the winter and spring semesters are re-examined during the September examination period.

03

Repeated failure

If a student fails more than three (3) times in the same course, the procedure defined by the current legislation is followed.

04

Requirements for the degree

To be awarded the Postgraduate Diploma, every student must successfully attend and pass examinations in all the mandatory courses offered by the programme and in a sufficient number of elective courses, in accordance with the course structure. In addition, each student must prepare and successfully defend a Master's Thesis, accumulating a total of 120 ECTS.

See the full course structure — semesters, mandatory and elective courses, ECTS distribution — in the curriculum overview, or read more about the Master's Thesis.
