Invitation to a Master thesis presentation

20.09.2025·News

Open presentation of a Master thesis by a graduating student of the programme.

All interested are invited to attend the presentation of the Master thesis of Evangelos Spyrou, graduating student of the Master's Programme in Geographic Information Systems.

The thesis is entitled “Development of an ArcGIS Pro ModelBuilder to automatically calculate morphometric indices: Application in Rhodes island, Greece”.

The presentation takes place online on Wednesday 24 September 2025, at 12:50, via Google Meet:

meet.google.com/jdn-uzfr-gwa

The three-member committee consists of Niki Evelpidou, Emmanuel Vassilakis and Charalambos Kranis.
