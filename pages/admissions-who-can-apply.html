Admissions

Who can apply

The Programme admits holders of a first-cycle degree from Greek universities, or from foreign institutions recognised by DOATAP, the Hellenic National Academic Recognition and Information Center. It is addressed primarily to graduates of the following schools and departments.

Recognition and places

Degrees awarded by foreign institutions must be recognised by DOATAP before they can be considered as a title of first-cycle studies for admission.

The number of places is set at fifty (50) students per academic year. Members of the ΕΕΠ, ΕΔΙΠ, and ΕΤΕΤ categories are additionally admitted as supernumeraries, in accordance with art. 34 par. 8 of Law 4485/2017.
