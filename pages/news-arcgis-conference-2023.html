ArcGIS Users of Greece and Cyprus Conference — workshop “Hands-on experience in GIS and MSc expertise — NKUA applications”

26.10.2023·Event

Programme-led workshop showcasing applied GIS work by NKUA students at the national ArcGIS users conference.

At the ArcGIS Users of Greece and Cyprus Conference, hosted by Marathon Data Systems on 24–25 October 2023, the Master's Programme in Geographic Information Systems ran a workshop entitled “Hands-on experience in GIS and MSc expertise — NKUA applications”.

Dr Giannis Saitis, who teaches on the Programme, presented the topics “Hands-on experience in GIS and MSc expertise — NKUA applications” and “Geomorphological Map of Naxos Island (1:50,000) Using Standard and Field Mapping Techniques”.

Panagiotis Dimopoulos, MSc student of the Programme and employee of Marathon Data Systems, gave a workshop on “ArcGIS Pro — 2D & 3D Desktop GIS Applications”.

Evangelos Spyrou, MSc student of the Programme and researcher at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, presented “ArcGIS Field Maps: Applications in Geomorphological Research — A Case Study”.

Dr Niki Evelpidou, Professor and Vice-President of the Programme, contributed to the presentation of the workshop topic, the geomorphological map of Naxos, and the ArcGIS Field Maps case study.
