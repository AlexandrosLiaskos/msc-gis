Master thesis

The 4th semester is devoted to the Master's thesis — an original, applied, research, or literature-review study on a topic relevant to the programme, written and defended in English before a three-member examination committee.

Semester
Fourth
Language
English
Committee
Three members
Deposit
PERGAMOS
  1. 01

    Proposal

    The Master's thesis is undertaken in the 4th semester. Assignment is made by the Coordinating Committee following an application co-signed by the candidate and the proposed supervisor, stating the proposed title and a summary of the intended work. To be assigned a subject, the student must have been successfully examined in all courses of the A and B semesters.

  2. 02

    Supervision

    The Coordinating Committee designates the supervisor and the other two members of the three-member examination committee. The supervisor and committee members are drawn from all categories of teaching staff of the programme, as defined in the internal regulation.

  3. 03

    Writing

    The subject of the thesis may have a practical-applied, academic-research or broad literature-review character, and must be relevant to the subject of the programme. The thesis must be written in English. A template is available for download to help structure the manuscript.

    Download the thesis template (DOCX) →
  4. 04

    Defence

    For the thesis to be approved, the student must defend it before the examination committee in a public presentation, held in English. The presentation must be announced on the programme website at least one week in advance. Once approved, the thesis and any accompanying material are deposited electronically in the University's digital repository, PERGAMOS, in accordance with decisions of the NKUA Senate.
