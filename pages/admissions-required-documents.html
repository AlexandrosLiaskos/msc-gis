Required of all applicants

  1. 01

    Application form

    Completed and signed application to the Programme.

    Download the application form (DOCX) →

  2. 02

    Curriculum vitae

  3. 03

    Degree or transcript

    Certified copy of degree, certificate of completion of studies, or detailed transcript if the first cycle of studies has not yet been completed.

  4. 04

    Identity card or passport

    Photocopy of both sides of the identity card, or of the passport.

  5. 05

    Two letters of recommendation

  6. 06

    Certificate of English language proficiency

    Level B2 or higher. All recognised English-language diplomas are accepted, as are degrees from English-speaking universities and transcripts listing courses taught in English.

  4. 10

    DOATAP recognition

    Foreign degrees

    Holders of degrees from foreign institutions must submit the certificate of equivalence from DOATAP, the Hellenic National Academic Recognition and Information Center, in accordance with art. 34 par. 7 of Law 4485/2017, as amended and in force.

  5. 11

    Transcript and formal declaration

    Final-year students

    Final-year students who expect to complete their studies before the application deadline submit a transcript and a formal declaration stating that they will provide the certificate of completion to the secretariat.
