How selection works

Applications are examined by the Coordinating Committee in order of protocol number, on the evidence of the application file alone — there is no entrance examination or interview. The Committee draws up a ranked evaluation table, ratified by the Faculty Assembly, on the basis of two sets of criteria:

The criteria

A. Primary criteria

  1. Degree grade, or current grade average if the first cycle of studies has not yet been completed (provided it is completed before enrolment in the Programme).
  2. Average grade in five (5) undergraduate courses related to the broader subject of the Programme.
  3. Performance in a thesis or internship, where one is provided for in the first cycle of studies.
  4. Certified knowledge of English at level B2 or higher.
  5. Letters of recommendation.

B. Secondary criteria

  1. Research activity or related professional work.
  2. Scientific publications.
  3. A second degree from the first or second cycle of studies.

Ranking and notification

In case of equal scores, tied candidates are admitted up to a limit not exceeding 10% of the maximum number of entrants.

Successful candidates are notified of their admission by email. Unsuccessful candidates are also notified by email, with the reason for the rejection clearly stated; objections and requests for re-evaluation may be lodged with the secretariat by email within five working days of notification.
