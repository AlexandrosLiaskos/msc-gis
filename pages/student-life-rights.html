Student life

Rights & obligations

Postgraduate students hold the same rights as first-cycle students, with a single exception, alongside a clear set of obligations — all defined in the programme's regulation.

Rights

  1. Full student rights

    Postgraduate students hold every right and benefit that first-cycle students enjoy — throughout their studies, including any granted extension — with the single exception of free textbooks.

  2. Accessibility support

    The University ensures that students with disabilities or special educational needs can access the course books and teaching, through its accessibility service (access.uoa.gr).

  3. Participation in academic life

    Students are welcome at the programme's research-group seminars, literature-review discussions, laboratory visits, conferences and workshops, lectures, and other scientific events.

  4. Diploma supplement

    Students may request a Diploma Supplement, issued in both Greek and English.

Obligations

Students are expected to meet the following obligations. The Faculty Assembly may remove a student from the programme who fails to meet them — or at the student's own request.

  1. Attendance

    Attend the courses and stay within the maximum absence limit set by the programme.

  2. Academic progress

    Pass the course examinations and complete the programme as set out in the regulation.

  3. Timely completion

    Complete your studies within the maximum duration defined in the regulation.

  4. Conduct

    Observe the University's rules of conduct; disciplinary matters are handled by the competent bodies.

  5. Tuition fees

    Pay the tuition fees within the deadlines set by the programme.
