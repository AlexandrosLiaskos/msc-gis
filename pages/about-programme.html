Programme

The Master's Programme in Geographic Information Systems is offered by the Faculty of Geology and Geoenvironment of the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens — a Faculty with long-standing experience in the management, presentation, and research use of spatial information.

01

Subject of study

The programme's field of study is Geographic Information Systems, with an emphasis on programming tools and databases in GIS environments, on web-based tools that support commercial applications, and on research at both applied and theoretical levels.

Its scope spans the geo-environment, geo-archaeology, medical geography, economics, and marketing — domains in which spatial analysis increasingly drives decision-making.

02

Purpose

The programme equips graduate scientists to meet the demands of new scientific challenges and technological requirements in fields that focus on spatial-information analysis and on the development of the tools that support it.

Graduates contribute to the development of the national economy by covering the growing need for trained specialists in the analysis of geographic data and in the management of environmental, cultural, socio-economic, and medical questions — through cartographic representation and spatial correlation.

03

Meet the faculty

The programme is led by a small core of Faculty members from the Department of Geology and Geoenvironment, whose research and teaching shape the curriculum from admissions through to thesis.
