01 Quality assurance policy Faculty of Geology and Geoenvironment · Document M1.1

The Faculty of Geology and Geoenvironment is committed to providing high-quality higher education, in Greek and in foreign languages, by organising and delivering Master's Programmes of high academic standards and significant added value in the key fields of the Faculty's science.

The Faculty pursues a creative environment for research, free scientific expression, and work for all staff and members of its academic community, and undertakes to apply this quality policy to every postgraduate programme it currently offers or will offer in the future, implementing the quality procedures foreseen by the University's Internal Quality Assurance System (ΕΣΔΠ) with the ultimate aim of continuous improvement.

Through this policy the academic identity and orientation of the Master's Programmes is strengthened, their purpose and subject are promoted, specific annual targets are pursued, the means and methods for achieving them are defined, and the participation of stakeholders in evaluation and redesign is safeguarded.

§ 1 Quality procedures

For the Master's Programme in Geographic Information Systems the Faculty applies the quality procedures set out in the University's Quality Manual, in close cooperation with the University's Quality Assurance Unit (ΜΟ.ΔΙ.Π.). All procedures are subject to annual inspection, review, and revision by the Internal Evaluation Committee (ΟΜΕΑ) in cooperation with ΜΟ.ΔΙ.Π.

  1. Implementation of the quality-assurance policy across all Master's Programmes, defining the framework within which they must operate.
  2. Allocation and management of the Programmes' resources, ensuring the personnel, funding, infrastructure, and equipment needed to operate the Programmes and to improve their effectiveness.
  3. Annual quality-assurance targets — specific, measurable, and observable indicators, agreed between Faculty and Programme leadership — through which the effectiveness of administrative, educational, and research operations is assessed.
  4. Periodic internal evaluation and annual monitoring of every Master's Programme, in cooperation with ΜΟ.ΔΙ.Π., identifying issues to correct and driving jointly agreed improvements.
  5. Collection of quality data — measurement, analysis, and improvement of the Programmes — feeding both the ΟΠΕΣΠ information system of ΕΘΑΑΕ and the internal information systems of the Faculty and the University with data on students, teaching staff, course structure, teaching, and student services.
  6. Public disclosure of information about the Master's Programmes — accessible, up-to-date, and objective content on a dedicated bilingual section of the Faculty's website, with defined review and update procedures.
  7. External evaluation of the Programmes through periodic accreditation by ΕΘΑΑΕ, conducted by committees of independent expert reviewers.

§ 2 What these procedures secure

Applied to the Master's Programme in Geographic Information Systems, these procedures secure:

  • The suitability of the Programme's structure and organisation.
  • Alignment of learning outcomes and graduate qualifications with the European and National Qualifications Framework, level 7.
  • The quality and effectiveness of teaching, monitored by the Programme's coordinating committee, ΟΜΕΑ, Faculty leadership, and ΜΟ.ΔΙ.Π.
  • That the qualifications of teaching staff — and especially of external collaborators — are appropriate for graduate education.
  • The drafting, application, and review of specific annual quality targets for the Programme.
  • The demand for graduates' qualifications in the labour market, tracked through regular market-needs research and follow-up on graduates' careers.
  • High-quality support services for graduate students — including students with disabilities — covering administrative services, libraries, online services, scholarships, and student-welfare services suited to the Programme.
  • Efficient use of the Programme's financial resources from tuition fees, funding both student services and Programme operations, including site visits outside the Faculty when needed for a complete education.
  • An annual internal review of the quality-assurance system for the Programme, conducted with the ΟΜΕΑ and ΜΟ.ΔΙ.Π.

§ 3 Annual quality targets

For the Master's Programme in Geographic Information Systems the Faculty focuses its efforts on the following targets:

  • Improving the graduation rate of postgraduate students.
  • Strengthening the culture of quality within the Programme.
  • Activating academic advising for students.
  • Active participation of students and graduates in internal-evaluation procedures.
  • Quality of the research output of Programme faculty, measured by publications and scholarly impact.
  • Involvement of Programme students in research activities.
  • Linking the Master's Programme with doctoral-level studies.
  • Actions connecting the Programme with the labour market and the local community.
  • Cooperation and networking with Faculty and Programme alumni.
  • Attracting international students to the Programme.
  • Attracting teaching staff from institutions abroad.
  • Participation in CIVIS — the European universities alliance.
  • Student participation in the ERASMUS programme.
  • Development of management information systems and digital-governance services.
  • Improvement of the infrastructure and support services for the student community.
  • Promotion of equal representation of the sexes.
