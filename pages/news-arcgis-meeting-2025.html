ArcGIS Users Meeting Greece – Cyprus 2025 — 22 & 23 October, Crowne Plaza Athens

10.06.2025·News

Two-day conference bringing together the ArcGIS user community from Greece and Cyprus.

The ArcGIS Users Meeting Greece – Cyprus 2025 will take place on 22 and 23 October 2025 at the Crowne Plaza Hotel in Athens. Participants are invited to submit a project or map and present their work to the community.

The annual gathering of the ArcGIS user community brings together practitioners from Greece and Cyprus around shared challenges, new solutions, and the capabilities of Esri technology.

The programme includes keynote speeches and user presentations, interactive technical workshops, exchange of experience and good practice, updates on the latest developments in the ArcGIS platform, and networking with GIS professionals from across Greece and Cyprus.
