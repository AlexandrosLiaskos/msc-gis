Open Presentation of a Master’s Thesis

12.09.2026·Event

The Master’s Programme “Geographic Information Systems” invites all interested parties to attend the open presentation of the Master’s Thesis by graduating student Eleni Poulakida.

The thesis is entitled: “Evaluating the Cooling Effect of Parks in Greece Using Satellite-Based Observations”.

The presentation takes place online on Saturday, 12 September 2026, at 10:30, via Webex.
