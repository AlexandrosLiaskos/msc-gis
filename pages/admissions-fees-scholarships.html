Fees & scholarships

Tuition for the Master's Programme in Geographic Information Systems is €5,000, paid in three instalments before the start of semesters A, B, and C. Fee waivers are available to students who meet the statutory economic, social, and academic-merit criteria.

Fee waivers & scholarships

Students of the programme are exempt from tuition fees when they meet the economic or social criteria and the conditions of academic excellence during their first cycle of studies, in accordance with the applicable legislation.

The application for a fee waiver under Law 4485/2017 is submitted after the selection process is completed and the names of the admitted students are posted on the ΔΙΑΥΓΕΙΑ transparency portal, following an announcement on this website which sets the submission window. The applicable procedure is described in Government Gazette 799 Bʹ/23-02-2022.
