Extension of the application deadline — new deadline Friday 26 September

12.09.2025·News

The application period has been extended by two weeks to give late applicants time to complete their file.

The deadline for submitting applications to the Master's Programme in Geographic Information Systems has been extended until Friday 26 September 2025.

All required documents are submitted by email to the Programme's Secretariat:
