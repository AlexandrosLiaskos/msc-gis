Winter-semester exam period 2024–⁠2025 — 26 January to 9 February

16.01.2025·News

Two-week examination window for the courses of the winter semester.

The examination period for the winter semester of the academic year 2024–2025 will run from 26 January until 9 February 2025.

Courses of the spring semester are scheduled to begin on Saturday 15 February 2025.
